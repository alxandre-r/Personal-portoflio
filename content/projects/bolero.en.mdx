## The problem: who is where, and when?

Weinmann Technologies' after-sales service planned technicians' jobs in Excel files. Outdated copies going around, broken formulas, and above all no readable view to answer a simple question: **where is each technician this week?**

Boléro is part of the [Weinmann Client Portal](/en/projects/espace-client-weinmann), the company's suite of internal tools.

## A "resource" view, not a calendar

A regular calendar shows events. The service team needed to see **people**. So I built the interface around DayPilot's Scheduler in resource mode:

- **one column per technician**, the hourly scale in rows;
- jobs are moved around with **drag & drop**;
- gaps, overlaps and routes are visible at a glance.

## An API shaped for scheduling

Showing several days of schedule for a whole team is a lot of requests if the API is naive. The Express back end exposes a **REST API designed for multi-day ranges**, so the view stays fast while scrolling through the week.

## Leaving Excel without losing history

The MySQL schema was designed properly from the start, and **migration scripts** brought the existing data over from the Excel exports.

## Going to production

Deployed on a server with **PM2** keeping the processes alive, and **Nginx** as a reverse proxy for SSL and subdomain routing.

<Callout title="Outcome">
V1 was adopted by the entire after-sales team, on desktop and tablet. Errors from broken Excel formulas are gone.
</Callout>
