## The floor first

Weinmann Technologies' warehouse staff prepared orders with paper sheets and spreadsheets: misreadings, lost information, and stock statuses reaching the ERP late.

I started from **observing them on the floor**, and that shaped everything else: they're standing, moving, a box in one hand and a phone in the other.

## Built for the thumb

The app is **mobile-first** in the strict sense: designed for a smartphone touch screen, then extended to the desktop.

## Talking to the ERP

Every stock update has to reach the ERP, in the right format, in real time. That job belongs to **Python micro-services** that format and forward the changes: the app stays simple, the integration stays isolated.

## When the Wi-Fi drops at the back of the warehouse

In a warehouse, connectivity is never guaranteed. Swing must not lose an entry because a request failed:

- submissions are **retried automatically** on failure;
- a **light local cache** holds state until the network comes back.

On the server, the MySQL schema is indexed for the queries warehouse staff actually run.

<Callout title="Outcome">
Paper sheets and spreadsheets are gone for good, and reading and transcription errors have all but disappeared.
</Callout>
