## Why Sandy

Everything about a car ends up scattered: fuel receipts in the glovebox, insurance in your inbox, maintenance in a spreadsheet nobody updates anymore. I wanted **one place** for all of it, and a way to share it with the rest of the household.

No existing app covered all of that smoothly: they were either too simplistic or built for professional fleets. So I built it myself, solo, from design to deployment.

<Callout title="Try it without signing up">
Sandy runs in production on Vercel and is **100% free**. **Demo mode** opens a ready-filled account (4 vehicles, 2 years of history, a family) so you can see every feature without creating an account: a 3-minute guided tour or free exploration, and everything resets in one click.

[Launch the demo](https://ma-voiture-sandy.vercel.app/demo) · [Open the app](https://ma-voiture-sandy.vercel.app/)
</Callout>

## A dashboard that answers real questions

*How much does my car really cost per kilometre? Is my fuel consumption drifting?* The dashboard is built around those questions rather than around raw data.

- expenses grouped by category: fuel, maintenance, insurance;
- consumption trends, cost per kilometre, period-over-period comparisons;
- multiple vehicles, switch between them in one click.

## Family sharing

A car is rarely used by just one person: a partner, kids borrowing it, parents. That's the need no existing app covered, and it's what makes Sandy a **household** tool rather than a simple logbook.

<Callout title="Share the car, not everything else">
Not everyone needs to be able to edit a fill-up or delete an expense. For **each vehicle**, the owner sets **each member's** rights: no access, read, or edit.
</Callout>

- you create a **family space** and invite your household with an invitation code;
- members find the shared vehicles and expenses in their own app;
- the dashboard can then analyse **the whole family's data** together, or a single vehicle;
- each member keeps their own visibility preferences, and the owner manages members and family settings.

## The document vault

Registration, insurance certificate, inspection report: stored once, and found on your phone the day you need them, at the roadside rather than in a drawer.

## Under the hood

Next.js and TypeScript for the interface, a Node.js back end, PostgreSQL for the data, deployed on Vercel.

Demo mode never touches the database: each visitor gets their own sandbox, rebuilt on the server from demo data, without seeing anything other visitors do.
